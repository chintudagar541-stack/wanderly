# Wanderly: animated shorts (Dagar Clips)

The animated story shorts live in `animated-shorts/` (Remotion + three.js, cartoon-3D style). Read
`animated-shorts/STYLE.md` before writing a script, building a world, or rendering. It holds the channel owner's
standing instructions. Follow them every time, without being asked again. Every world must be realistic: read
`animated-shorts/REALISM.md` and build sets from the shared library `animated-shorts/src/lib/realism/`.

Talk to the owner in Hinglish (Roman-script Hindi + English), short and clear.

To make a new video, follow the `reference-short` skill (`.claude/skills/reference-short/SKILL.md`). It covers the
full pipeline. The template is `animated-shorts/src/videos/diwali2/`. For ideas or scripts, use the `story-script` skill.
