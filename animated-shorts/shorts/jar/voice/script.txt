An ancient king owned a jar that could predict wars.
Nobody knew where it came from.
But whenever an enemy army was preparing to attack, the jar would make a strange cracking sound.
The first time it happened, the king ignored it.
Three days later, an army invaded his kingdom.
The second time, he listened.
He moved his soldiers to the border, and his kingdom survived.
Soon, the king began trusting the jar more than his own generals.
But one night, something terrifying happened.
The jar started cracking.
Once.
Twice.
Then it shattered.
The king immediately ordered his entire army to prepare for war.
But there was no enemy.
No army.
No approaching soldiers.
Days passed.
Then weeks.
And nothing happened.
Until one morning, the king looked into his own castle courtyard.
His generals were standing there.
His soldiers were surrounding the palace.
And every single one of them was pointing their weapons at him.
The jar hadn't predicted an invasion.
It had predicted a rebellion.
