In two thousand and two, a town in England elected a monkey as mayor.
Hartlepool.
A proud old port with one embarrassing legend: two hundred years ago, locals supposedly found a monkey from a French shipwreck... and put it on trial as a spy.
So the football club made a monkey its mascot.
Inside the suit: a twenty-eight-year-old called Stuart.
When the town held its first-ever vote for mayor, Stuart entered.
As the monkey.
For the publicity.
One promise: free bananas for schoolchildren.
No campaign. No plan. Just the suit.
First count: the monkey's in front. By two hundred and fifty-eight votes.
Second count... the monkey wins.
Luckily, Stuart took the job seriously.
Unluckily, he wasn't allowed to wear the suit to work.
How did a joke win?
Every voter picked a favourite and a backup.
It's like ordering pizza for a party: if your favourite's sold out, your backup still counts.
Nobody got half, so everyone else's backups were counted... and more of them said "monkey".
The bananas were too expensive, so schools got free fruit instead.
Three years later, Hartlepool re-elected him by more than ten thousand votes.
Then again.
The first mayor in Britain to win a third term.
In twenty-twelve, the town voted to scrap the job completely.
So the only mayor Hartlepool ever elected... started out as a monkey.
