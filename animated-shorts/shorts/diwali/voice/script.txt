Every year, millions of people light tiny oil lamps, decorate their homes, and celebrate Diwali.
But have you ever wondered why?
The story goes back thousands of years…
According to the Hindu epic Ramayana, a prince named Rama was sent into exile for 14 years.
His wife Sita and brother Lakshmana went with him.
But during their exile, Sita was kidnapped by the demon king Ravana.
Rama eventually defeated Ravana and rescued her.
After 14 long years, Rama finally returned to his kingdom, Ayodhya.
And the people were so happy to see their king return…
They lit thousands of oil lamps to illuminate the entire city and welcome him home.
That is why, in this tradition, people light diyas during Diwali.
But Diwali isn’t just about celebrating a king’s return.
It’s also a celebration of hope, new beginnings, and the victory of light over darkness.
And that’s the beautiful reason…
Why even today, thousands of years later, millions of tiny flames still light up the night.
