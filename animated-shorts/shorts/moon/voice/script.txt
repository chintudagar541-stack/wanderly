Imagine waking up one morning and discovering that you own the Moon.
Sounds impossible, right?
Well, that's exactly what happened to a man named Kevin.
One night, Kevin was browsing an online auction website when he found something interesting.
"Moon for Sale. Starting Price: ten dollars."
Thinking it was just a stupid joke, Kevin placed a bid of ten dollars.
But the next morning, he received an email.
Congratulations! You are now the legal owner of the Moon.
Kevin thought it was a scam.
Until three days later, a lawyer showed up at his apartment with forty seven pages of ownership documents.
Apparently, the previous owner had sold the Moon to pay off his gambling debts.
Kevin was now officially a billionaire in real estate.
Well, technically, he owned a giant rock.
But things got worse.
NASA contacted him and asked for permission to land on his property.
Kevin refused.
Unless they paid rent.
NASA offered him five hundred dollars.
Kevin demanded five million dollars.
Negotiations went nowhere.
Then Kevin made his biggest mistake.
He announced that he was going to divide the Moon into smaller properties and sell them.
Within hours, thousands of people started buying imaginary lunar apartments.
One man even complained that his Moon apartment had no parking space.
But then, the government stepped in.
They explained that buying the Moon online didn't actually make him its owner.
Kevin was devastated.
He had already spent his entire savings designing a luxury hotel on the Moon.
And the worst part?
He couldn't even get a refund.
So now Kevin is back to his normal job.
But every night, he looks up at the sky and whispers...
"That could have been a really expensive Airbnb."
