One morning, a completely ordinary man named Kevin woke up and discovered that he was the mayor of a town.
The problem?
He had never even visited that town.
Apparently, the previous mayor had resigned, the election committee had made a mistake, and Kevin's name was accidentally selected from a list of people who owed the town money.
And somehow, everyone believed he was qualified.
On his first day, Kevin was asked to solve three problems.
The town had no electricity, the roads were full of holes, and the local ducks had somehow taken over the public swimming pool.
Kevin looked at the situation and made his first official decision.
He declared the ducks the new security department.
Surprisingly, crime dropped by forty percent.
People started loving him.
Within a week, the town had electricity, repaired roads, and ducks wearing tiny security hats.
But then came the next election.
Kevin received ninety eight percent of the votes.
He was confused.
Until he discovered who the other two percent were.
The ducks.
And they had already picked their own candidate.
