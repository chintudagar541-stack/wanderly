A man once sold his shadow for one million dollars.
And the strangest part?
The shadow actually belonged to him.
It started when a mysterious businessman approached him in an empty street, and made him an offer.
One million dollars.
All he had to do was stand under a strange machine for ten seconds.
The man laughed, thinking it was a joke.
But when he stepped out of the machine, his shadow was gone.
And the money was real.
At first, life was perfect.
He bought a mansion, expensive cars, and traveled around the world.
But after a few days, something terrifying happened.
Every night, he noticed a dark figure standing outside his bedroom window.
It looked exactly like him.
Except it had no face.
He called the police, but whenever they arrived, the figure disappeared.
One night, he finally saw it clearly.
His shadow was standing inside his bedroom.
And it was smiling.
The next morning, he tracked down the businessman, and demanded his shadow back.
But the businessman simply opened a laptop.
On the screen was a video of the machine.
And inside it, the man's shadow was moving, on its own.
Then the businessman said something terrifying.
You didn't sell your shadow. You sold the only thing that was keeping your other self trapped.
Suddenly, every light in the room went out.
And from behind him, a familiar voice whispered...
Finally. My turn.
