The zombie apocalypse reached Dry Gulch on a Tuesday. Very, very slowly.

At sunrise, the lookout spotted one zombie, a mile outside town. The sheriff rang the bell. The town boarded up every window, loaded every shotgun, and waited.

By noon, the zombie had reached the cactus. By sunset, it was stuck on the cactus.

On day two, the town got bored. The saloon started taking bets. Someone opened a lemonade stand, right on the zombie's route. The zombie bought a lemonade.

On day three, it finally reached the gate. The whole town took aim. The zombie groaned, raised a rotting hand, and bit the sheriff. Gently. It had no teeth.

The doctor took one look, and lowered his glasses. It wasn't a zombie. It was Ezra Pike. He'd walked all the way back from Boot Hill, to complain about the service.
