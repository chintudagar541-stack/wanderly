# Molasses Tank: script, shots and ideas

Format: 67 s vertical (9:16), deadpan narration, one real physical cause, callback ending.

## Facts (check before you publish)
- Boston, 15 January 1919. Tank owned by the Purity Distilling Co. (part of United States Industrial Alcohol), about 50 ft tall and 90 ft wide.
- About 2.3 million gallons of molasses. Fermentation produced gas; the temperature rose sharply in the two days before.
- Wave reported about 25 ft high at roughly 35 mph. It damaged buildings and the elevated railway.
- 21 people died, about 150 were injured.
- Fire boats pumped salt water; the harbour ran brown into summer.
- The company blamed anarchists. The court found the company responsible; the tank was not properly tested or built.
- "Some say the smell lingers on hot days" is local legend, so the script says "Some say".
